✦ FAQs

Questions about My Seven Ocean

My Seven Ocean is a marine skincare and fragrance distribution brand. Here are the answers to what retailers and customers ask us most.

01What is My Seven Ocean?

My Seven Ocean is a specialized distribution house bringing authentic, marine-derived formulations (bioluminescent serums, peptide barrier creams, glacial mineral mists, and luxury extrait flacons) from certified ocean laboratories directly to verified regional retailers and end-clients.

02Are you a wholesale distributor or do you support direct retail?

Both. We operate as an authorized master distributor supplying luxury spas, beauty boutiques, and department stores in batch cases, while also fulfilling reserved direct customer orders seamlessly via our interactive Ocean Bag platform.

03How do retailers apply for wholesale & distribution partnerships?

Simply reach out via our contact channels or reserve an initial collection order. Our dedicated trade accounts team provides tailored tiered pricing margins, guaranteed batch priority, certified cold-chain transit, and complete promotional visual collateral within 24 hours.

04What territories and geographic zones do you ship to?

We supply partners internationally across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. All consignments travel inside thermal aqua-insulated protective crates with automated real-time customs telemetry tracking.

05How do you guarantee formulation authenticity & skin safety?

Every single bottle and jar carries a micro-embossed holographic serial seal linked to lab-verified certificate of analysis (COA). All marine peptides and algal extracts are dermatologically audited, heavy-metal tested, and cruelty-free certified.

06What is the consignment guarantee and return protocol?

We offer a zero-friction 7-day transit damage replacement guarantee. Any unsealed defect or misrouted case is replaced immediately via express air freight at zero additional cost to our retail partners.
