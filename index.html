✦ Wholesale & Distribution FAQs

Questions about My Seven Ocean

MY SEVEN OCEAN LLC is a US-based wholesale e-commerce and authorized brand distribution company. Here are answers to the most common questions from brand owners and suppliers.

01What is MY SEVEN OCEAN LLC?

MY SEVEN OCEAN LLC is a US-based wholesale e-commerce and brand distribution enterprise specializing in direct partnerships with established beauty, skincare, and consumer brands. We work with 50+ brands across 10+ active Amazon USA accounts, generating over $20,000 in daily sales volume.

02How do you protect brand equity and MAP pricing?

We enforce 100% strict Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) compliance across every product we distribute. Our goal is never short-term discounting—we protect your brand's perceived value, monitor listing health, and ensure consistent pricing integrity across all marketplaces.

03How do brand owners open a wholesale distribution account with you?

Brand owners, manufacturers, and authorized master suppliers can connect directly with our partnerships representative Huzaifa at hello@mysevenocean.com. We review your catalog, establish recurring bulk purchase orders, and handle all downstream warehousing and FBA prep.

04What marketplaces and territories do you distribute in?

Our core operations focus on the Amazon USA marketplace alongside verified North American retail and e-commerce distribution channels. All inventory is managed through climate-controlled US prep centers and Amazon FBA fulfillment networks.

05How do you guarantee product authenticity and quality control?

We source 100% directly from brand owners and authorized manufacturers with full invoice chain-of-custody, batch tracking, and expiration date auditing so end customers always receive fresh, factory-sealed products.

06How do you prevent stockouts on high-velocity SKUs?

Our supply chain team uses predictive demand forecasting and automated reorder triggers across our multi-warehouse network to maintain uninterrupted stock availability and preserve top Buy Box placement.
